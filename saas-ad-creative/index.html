05 / BEFORE WE START

A few practical
questions.

Do I need to come up with the ad messages?

No. You provide the brief and confirm product facts. I propose three messages; you select two before I design the concepts.

Which sizes will I receive?

Two agreed sizes for each of the two selected concepts, for one advertising platform. We confirm the dimensions in the brief. The final delivery is four PNGs.

Are editable source files included?

No. This pilot is a PNG-only delivery. If your workflow requires editable files, discuss that requirement before booking rather than assuming they are included.

Do you use AI in the creative process?

Yes. The creative production is AI-assisted. Product facts and claims must be confirmed by you before they are used in the final creative.

Are the portfolio brands your clients?

No. The work shown here is independent concept work, not commissioned by the brands. The monday service samples are not affiliated with monday.com. There are no campaign performance results for these concepts.

Do you run the ads or guarantee results?

No. I provide creative materials for testing. Campaign setup, media buying, ad management and performance analysis are outside the pilot. Clicks, leads, sales and other campaign results are not guaranteed.

Can we work together after the pilot?

If the collaboration is a good fit, we can agree a separate fixed monthly package for fresh messages and static creative. Its price, volume and turnaround are not included in this pilot.
